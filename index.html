Weighted average of the seven 0–100 maps. Weights: observed 28%, draw pace 18%, OECD cover 14%, US SPR 14%, US commercial 8%, products 10%, floating/on-water 8%. The number on the desk is the arithmetic — not a call.

Why the print is not 90

IEA observed stocks are about 7.80 billion barrels at end-August. That is below the pre-war ~8.3B and into a tight zone. It is not the May 2026 JPMorgan operational floor of 6.8B, which assumed no resolution and 5.6 mb/d of demand destruction. IEA now has 2026 demand −2.5 mb/d.

Split personality

US commercial crude is seasonal-normal. The SPR is a four-decade low. August's observed draw was mostly oil-on-water. OECD commercial tanks rose while governments drew. Diesel, not generic crude tanks, is the binding constraint.

Hard rule

No interpolated weekly IEA global billion-barrel series. JPM 7.6 / 6.8 lines are a reference path, not measured physics. Yellow inputs change only when a named publisher prints.
